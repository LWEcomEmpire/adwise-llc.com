The short version.

  • This is a static website. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising pixels or other tracking.
  • It loads nothing from third parties: fonts, styles and scripts are all served from this domain.
  • If you email us, we use your message only to reply and, if we work together, to provide our services.

01Who we are

This website, adwise-llc.com (the “Site”), is operated by ADWISE LLC (“ADWISE”, “we”, “us”), a performance-marketing agency for ecommerce brands located at 10225 Ulmerton Rd, 3D, Largo, FL 33771, United States. This policy explains what information is handled when you visit the Site, when you contact us, and when we provide services to clients.

02Visiting this website

The Site is a static website with no user accounts, no contact forms and no comment features. We do not:

  • set cookies or use similar technologies such as local storage for tracking;
  • use analytics tools, advertising pixels, session recording or fingerprinting;
  • load fonts, scripts, styles or other resources from third-party servers;
  • collect personal information through the Site itself.

The Site uses a small script for interface features only, such as the mobile menu and the “copy email” button. It does not send any data anywhere.

03Hosting

The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like most web hosts, GitHub may automatically log technical information about requests to the Site, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request, for security and operational purposes. That processing is carried out by GitHub under its own privacy statement, available at docs.github.com. We do not use hosting data to identify, profile or track visitors.

04Emails you send us

If you email us at lw.adwise@gmail.com, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and anything you choose to write or attach. We use this information only to reply to you and, if we agree to work together, to provide our services and keep ordinary business records.

We do not add you to mailing lists, and we do not use your message for advertising. Messages are stored with our email provider.

05Client data during engagements

When a business engages us, we may access its advertising accounts, analytics, store and product data and related business information through access the client grants. We process this data only to deliver the agreed services and according to the client’s instructions. Clients can remove our access at any time.

Where that data includes personal information about the client’s own customers, such as audience lists or order data, we handle it on the client’s behalf, use it only to provide the services and do not use it for any purpose of our own. If a client and ADWISE sign a separate agreement covering data processing, that agreement applies.

06Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only with service providers we need to operate (such as our hosting and email providers), when a client instructs us to as part of the services, or when required by law.

07Retention and security

We keep emails for as long as needed to respond, to provide our services and to meet legal and accounting obligations. Access to client accounts ends when an engagement ends or when the client removes it, and we delete or return client data on request unless we are legally required to keep it. We use reasonable measures to protect the information we hold, including access controls on the accounts we use.

08Your rights

Depending on where you live, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and California, you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we hold about you and receive a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate information corrected;
  • have your information deleted;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • not be treated differently for exercising any of these rights.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on our legitimate interest in answering messages sent to us, and on the performance of a contract (or steps taken before entering one) when we provide services. To exercise any right, email us at lw.adwise@gmail.com. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

If your request concerns data we process on behalf of one of our clients, we will forward it to that client or help them respond.

09International transfers

ADWISE is based in the United States. If you contact us from another country, your information will be processed in the United States and in the countries where our service providers operate.

10Children

The Site and our services are intended for businesses. They are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the effective date at the top.
