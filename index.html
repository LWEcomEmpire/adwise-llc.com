02 Method

A system, not a set of hunches.

Five working habits behind every account we run. None of them is a secret. The difference is doing all of them, every day.

  1. A

    Creative engine

    Ads wear out. We keep a steady supply of new ones.

    High-volume production of new concepts, hooks and formats, AI-assisted for speed and directed by people for taste and accuracy. Winning concepts are localized natively for each language and market: rewritten and re-voiced, not just subtitled.

    Localize natively. Never just translate.

  2. B

    Structured testing

    Every launch answers one clear question.

    A new hook, a new angle, a new product, a new market: before anything goes live we write down what it is testing. Test budgets are fixed in advance and kill rules are set before day one, so a losing test ends on schedule and a winner is recognized for what it is.

    One launch, one question.

  3. C

    Scale on true profit

    We steer by margin, not by the ad dashboard.

    In-platform ROAS leaves out product cost, shipping, payment fees and refunds, and every platform measures itself. We decide what to scale on real margin, calculated from your own store data after all of those costs.

    Margin decides. The dashboard advises.

  4. D

    Market expansion

    Proven winners travel.

    When a creative and an offer prove themselves in one market, we replicate them in new countries and languages, with localized copy and creative and the same profit rules applied to each new market.

    Prove it once, then take it abroad.

  5. E

    Daily monitoring

    Watched every day, with alarms on.

    Accounts are reviewed daily, and automated alerts flag what should not wait: spend spikes, broken tracking, rejected ads, campaigns drifting below their margin target. Problems are caught early, not at the end-of-month review.

    Watched daily. Alerted automatically.

03 Process

From first email to a running account.

A short, written path from audit to scale. You always know what we are doing, why, and what we are measuring it against.

  1. 01

    Audit

    We go through your ad accounts, tracking, product feed, past creative and unit economics: what each order really costs you to make, ship and sell.

    You getA written audit and the cost model we will measure against.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Channels, markets, creative angles, test budgets and kill rules, agreed in writing before anything is spent.

    You getA launch plan with one question for every test.

  3. 03

    Launch

    Clean campaign structure, creative ready, tracking verified and alerts switched on before the first euro or dollar goes out.

    You getLive campaigns in ad accounts you own.

  4. 04

    Optimize & scale

    Daily monitoring and a steady flow of new creative. Losing tests are cut by the rules; winners earn budget and new markets.

    You getDecisions made on margin, and written down.

  5. 05

    Report

    Plain-language reporting on spend, revenue and true profit by channel and market, plus what we are testing next.

    You getA clear view of what is working, and why.

Optimize, scale and report repeat for as long as we work together.

04 FAQ

Straight answers.

Something else on your mind? Email us and ask.

Which platforms do you run?

Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Pinterest, TikTok and Google, including Search, Shopping, Performance Max and YouTube. We can run a single channel or several together, all judged by the same profit numbers.

Do you produce the ad creatives?

Yes. Creative production is built into how we work: new concepts and variations on an ongoing basis, AI-assisted and localized for each market. If you have product footage, an in-house team or creators, we work with their material too.

Do you work with brands outside the US?

Yes. We work with ecommerce brands selling in the US, Europe and other English-speaking markets, and we localize campaigns and creative for each country and language we advertise in.

Who owns the ad accounts?

You do. Ad accounts, pixels, catalogs and data stay in your business. We work through partner access that you grant from your own business settings, and you can remove it at any time.

What do you need from us to start?

Access to your ad accounts and store analytics, and your real cost data: product cost, shipping, fees and refunds. Without those we can only measure ROAS; with them we can measure profit.

How do we get started?

Email us at contact@adwise-llc.com with your store URL, the markets you sell in and the channels you run today. We will reply with next steps and set up a first call.
